    How it works: a series is 10 rounds back to back — each round winner scores +1 win in the standings, the next round starts on its own, and when the whole series ends a brand-new series starts automatically after a short countdown, so the live never stops. The pace is deliberately slow and intense: marbles cruise slower and every cut is small, so each round is a long fight that is worn down slice by slice. Every marble wears its country flag and stays small and fixed-size the whole battle — it never grows. Each country’s strength is its line (50): the coloured fan sweeping behind a marble is a blade, and when it slices through an enemy, that enemy’s line gets cut (watch for the ✂ hits). When a country’s line is fully cut down to 0, it is eliminated. The winner is always random — your pick is only a guess and changes nothing, the leader gets ganged up on, and last round’s winner starts the next round at 44 line so nobody collects crowns back to back. After 95 seconds, Sudden Death doubles every cut and the arena walls slowly close in. Every 10–20 seconds a natural English voice calls out — “Double tap to power up your country!” or “Which country are you watching from? Comment your country!” — to keep the live audience hooked.
